Accounting and advisory firms: documents and deadlines

We can review how documents arrive and what is missing from each job, keeping your team in charge of review.

  • Collecting documentsOrganize files and remind people about missing items through channels that can be connected.
  • Reading invoices and receiptsExplore data extraction with human review and import into your accounting software, where supported.
  • Client deadline remindersPrepare reminders using dates and recipients checked by your team.

Property managers: issues and follow-up

We can look at bringing reports and responses together so you can see who owns each issue and its current status.

  • Logging issuesKeep the owner, date, and status together, with automatic or manual entry depending on the channel.
  • Following up with vendors and residentsExplore notifications based on status changes confirmed by the person responsible.
  • Preparing communicationsExplore templates and reminders with recipients and content reviewed before sending.

Renovation and installation: inquiries and quotes

We can explore how to collect requests that arrive while you are on site, so you do not have to piece together each inquiry from messages and photos.

  • Collecting requestsConsider a form or connected channel to gather details, photos, and measurements for review.
  • Drafting quotesExplore templates based on your rates, with prices and scope reviewed by you before sending.
  • Tracking inquiries and visitsLook at reminders for open requests and notifications for confirmed appointments.

Questions about automation and our first conversation

You do not need technical knowledge or a solution in mind.

Is the first conversation free?

Yes. It helps me understand how you work and whether there is a problem I can help with. It does not commit you to an assessment, prototype, or development project.

What if I don't know what can be automated?

That is fine. We can start with a recent job: how it came in, who was involved, and where problems arose. You know the business; I investigate the technical options.

What should I prepare or share?

Think of a task that repeats or causes problems. If we need an example, use fictional or anonymized data. You do not need to send passwords, customer documents, or sensitive information.

Will we need to replace our existing software?

Not necessarily. First we check whether work can be simplified with what you already use. Connecting tools depends on their features, permissions, and costs, so we need to check each case.

Will there be a prototype after the conversation?

Not always. If we find a specific problem and a test would help us evaluate an improvement, we agree on what to test and the terms before starting. A prototype helps us learn; it would not be a complete solution ready for production.

When do we discuss pricing?

The initial conversation is free. If continuing makes sense, we agree on the next piece of work and its terms before starting. Any subsequent development or maintenance would have a proposal and quote for you to accept or decline.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. I am based in Madrid and work remotely with businesses internationally. We use video calls, email, and examples of your processes to define the work and review progress. We agree on meeting times that work for both of us.
